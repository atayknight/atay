      Atay Studio
      Atay Studio is the film studio of Ayoub Ouazzou (Atay Knight), a director of photography, video editor, colorist and 3D/VFX artist based in Marrakech, Morocco. We shoot and finish tourism films, commercials, social campaigns, festival recaps and hotel films across Morocco, and work remotely with clients worldwide, in Arabic, English and French.
      
 
      Services
      • Cinematography and DOP
      • Video editing
      • Colour grading
      • 3D, motion and VFX
      • Social reels and vertical campaigns
      • Tourism, hotel and destination films
